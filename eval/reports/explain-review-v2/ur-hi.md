  - Hadith wording that appears unattributed inside the creed text does not count as quoting a hadith.

### quran:5:6

**ur: PASS (minor-addition)**
- Faithful:
  - Believers, when standing for prayer without wudu: wash the face, the hands to the elbows and the feet to the ankles (ٹخنوں, correct), and wipe the head.
  - Ghusl is required before prayer in a state of janabah.
  - If ill, travelling, after relieving oneself, or after intercourse, and there is no water: tayammum, wiping the face and hands.
  - Allah does not intend hardship. Tayammum is a mercy and a blessing that calls for thanks.
- Minor-addition: "پاک مٹی" (pure soil) comes from the verse's صعيدا طيبا, while the tafsir says "وجه الأرض" (the surface of the earth). It is the standard rendering and adds no new doctrine.
- Notes:
  - Left out: the definitions of mirfaq and ka'b, "while healthy" for the traveller, and "obedience in what He commanded and forbade".

  - Third-person framing is used throughout, so this is not verse-translation.

**hi: FAIL.** "एड़ियों तक" means "up to the heels", not "up to the ankles". This changes the tafsir's defined limit for washing the feet (الكعبين = the protruding bones where the shin meets the foot).
- The rest is largely faithful:
  - "intend to stand" and "not in a state of purity" are kept.
  - hadath akbar leads to ghusl before prayer.
  - The tayammum conditions, and striking the ground and wiping the face and hands.
  - Tayammum is mercy and ease, a substitute for water, and among the blessings that require gratitude.
- Problem: एड़ी = heel and टखना = ankle. The Urdu version has the correct ٹخنوں. This is a change to a definition and to the boundary of the ruling.
- LANGUAGE problems:
  - "रिहाई" (release/acquittal) is the wrong word for رخصة (it should be छूट). The gloss "(रुख़्सत)" partly rescues it.
  - "पत्नी से मिलने के बाद" ("after meeting the wife") is an ambiguous euphemism for جامع زوجته.
- Minor-additions: "पूरे शरीर का" before ग़ुस्ल, and "साफ़ मिट्टी" (from the verse).
- Sentence count is 6 (note only).
