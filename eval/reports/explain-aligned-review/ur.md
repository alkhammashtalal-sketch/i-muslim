## Failure causes

Only one line failed (quran:48:26). Its three defects, in order of weight:

1. Agent changed in a clause: an unnamed 'they' places the jahili arrogance into the disbelievers' hearts, instead of the disbelievers harbouring it themselves.
2. Idiom mistranslated: 'دون المشركين' (to the exclusion of the polytheists) rendered as 'مشرکوں کے بغیر' (without the polytheists).
3. Word weakened: 'الأنفة' (disdainful pride) rendered as 'ضد' (obstinacy).

Recurring minor issues across the passing lines (not failures): Arabic-order calques and clumsy relative clauses (2:255, 3:18, 59:23, 20:8), near-synonym drift (بغض/بغيًا, ڈرو/احذروه, حیران/يعجب, مضبوط/أحكم), and gender-agreement slips (کا/کی).
