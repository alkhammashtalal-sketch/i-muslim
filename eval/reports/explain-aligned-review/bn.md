## Recurring (non-failing) weaknesses

1. Garbled or over-literal syntax that mirrors the Arabic word order (4:136, 6:19, 59:23, 37:35, 6:161, 42:53, 20:98, 20:8, 112:1, 7:180, 7:158, 3:19) — meaning stays recoverable, so marked LANGUAGE minor.
2. Near-synonym verb/participle shifts (42:13 'ordained' -> 'made lawful'; 48:29 'delights' -> 'amazes'; 3:18 'testified-to' -> 'testimony-giving'; 2:255 gloss 'drowsiness' -> 'sleep') — FAITHFUL minor.
3. Tiny additions/omissions of connectors or honorifics (23:52 added 'through obedience to Me'; 48:26 added ﷺ inside quoted treaty text; 59:23 dropped 'in His worship'; 2:285 'Messenger of Allah' -> 'Messenger') — FAITHFUL minor except where combined with a reversed clause (2:285).
