## Failures

- **quran:2:255** — S2-S3: الكرسي rendered 'Trono' (Throne, = العرش) — 'Su Trono (Kursi) ... el Trono (Kursi) es el lugar de los pies del Señor' conflates al-Kursi with the Throne, contradicting the Muyassar's own gloss that the Kursi is the place of the two feet; 'Versículo del Trono' repeats it. S1: 'Dios, que no merece la divinidad y la adoración sino Él' is garbled — reads as God not deserving divinity (Arabic: none deserves divinity and worship except Him). S2: 'soberbia' (arrogance) for الكبرياء

## Most common failure causes

Only one line failed, so the "most common" causes are the three defects inside it, in order of weight:

1. Islamic-term conflation: الكرسي (al-Kursi) rendered 'Trono' (Throne), which in Spanish Muslim usage denotes العرش; the Muyassar explicitly glosses the Kursi as the place of the two feet, so the label contradicts the gloss it sits next to (quran:2:255 S2-S3).
2. Garbled relative clause for 'الذي لا ... إلا هو': 'Dios, que no merece la divinidad y la adoración sino Él' reads as God not deserving divinity (quran:2:255 S1). The same construction is clumsy but still recoverable in quran:20:98, which passed as minor.
3. Negative-connotation vocabulary for divine attributes: 'soberbia' / 'el Soberbio' for الكبرياء / المتكبِّر (quran:2:255 S2; tolerated as minor in quran:59:23 because the explanatory clause fixes the sense).

## Recurring minor issues (did not cause failure)

- Added transliteration glosses in parentheses — (tawhid), (Ibrahim), (muttaqin), (julla), (salat), (zakat), (jahiliyya), (Ar-Rahmán), (Ar-Rahim) — harmless clarifications, never new content.
- Grammatical-person slips (singular 'te/tú' for the Messenger rendered as plural 'os/vosotros') in quran:42:13; a misattached preposition in quran:7:158.
- Over-literal or slightly off word choices: 'cálculo' for الحساب, 'semilla' for زرع, 'benefactor' for محسن, 'bondad' for الحسن, 'levantaos' for قوموا, 'recompensados' for يجزون.
- Small grammar errors: 'la mayor testimonio', 'hace existir a ellos', 'le decís a mí', 'era de todo conocimiento'.
- Register alternates between 'vosotros' (most lines) and 'ustedes' (quran:2:21); each line is internally consistent.
- Rendering of الله alternates between 'Dios', 'Alá', 'Allah' and 'Al-lah' across lines; all are accepted Spanish forms, but the corpus is not uniform.
