## Failures

- **quran:2:21** — s2: 'أوجدكم من العدم' (brought you into being from non-existence) rendered 'अस्तित्व से पैदा किया' (created you from existence) - reversed meaning
- **quran:64:13** — 'المؤمنون بوحدانيته' (those who believe in His oneness) rendered 'ईमानवालों को अपनी एकता में' (believers, in their own unity) - qualifier changed, new idea added
- **quran:3:18** — 'على أجلِّ مشهود عليه' (upon the greatest matter testified to) rendered 'सबसे महान गवाही देने वाले पर' (upon the greatest witness-giver) - witness/witnessed reversed

## Most common failure causes

1. Mistranslated key phrase that reverses meaning (2:21 'from non-existence' → 'from existence'; 3:18 'the thing witnessed' → 'the witness').
2. Qualifier misattached, introducing an idea not in the source (64:13 'believers in His oneness' → 'believers, in their own unity').
3. No TERMS or LANGUAGE failures; recurring non-fatal issues were Arabic-like word order, malformed Islamic-term spellings (शरईअतों, शरियों, दालालत, अल्लाहियत) and misplaced 'سبحانه'.
