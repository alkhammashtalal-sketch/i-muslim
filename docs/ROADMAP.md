# Roadmap after 1.1 (add-on 0072-add26, Talal 25 October 12:33: «كلها أعجبتني، بنسويها كلها»)

Plans only. Each release goes through the same rules as before: the constant sources (CLAUDE.md §2), an independent review of every new religious text, and Talal's approval.

## 1.1.1 (right after 1.1.0)
- The user's data kept by the phone's own storage (`@capacitor/preferences`), migrated once from the WebView's `localStorage` (add-on 0072-add25).
- The full adhan of each prayer inside the app (add-on 0072-add24 §1).
- The app's link and a QR code at the bottom of the share images (add-on 0072-add26 §3).
- Asking for a rating at the right moment, at most once in 120 days (add-on 0072-add26 §5).

## 1.2: Ramadan (sent before mid-January 2027)
- The month's timetable from the app's own prayer times. The end of suhur is Fajr itself, with no «imsak» minutes before it unless a text from the sources says otherwise.
- The iftar and suhur countdown on the lock screen and the Dynamic Island (Live Activity).
- Taraweeh and qiyam al-layl: the prayers lesson's batch 2, with its own independent review.
- The fasting adhkar from our data with their grades; what is weak is not shown as a hadith.
- In-App Events on the App Store: «Ramadan with Muslim» and «The ten days of Dhul-Hijjah». Talal submits them himself.
- Plan and files: `relay/w1/0072-ramadan/` and `relay/w1/0072-store/events/` (outside git).

## 1.3: before Dhul-Hijjah 1448
- A guide to Hajj and Umrah step by step, in the style of the prayer lesson and with its review.

## After 1.3
- An Apple Watch app: the tasbih, the prayer times, and watch complications.
