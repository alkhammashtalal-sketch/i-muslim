New in version 1.1

On the web now, and in the iPhone app with its 1.1 update.

  • The full adhan of each prayer on the prayer times page, played when you tap it.
  • Prayer times by your country’s method automatically, which you can change, with the time when voluntary prayer is not offered marked.
  • Ramadan: the month’s times with the length of each day’s fast, the end of suhoor at the Fajr adhan, the countdown to iftar, and the adhkar of fasting. Its page appears from 1 Sha’ban.
  • «Ask» is more precise: it understands questions in more languages, and answers from authentic hadiths with their grade and source.
  • Tafsir Ibn Kathir beside al-Muyassar and al-Saadi, and a «Listen» button under the texts: the ayah in the recitation of the reciter you choose.
  • «Teach me ablution», and «Teach me the prayer» for each of the five prayers, step by step with its evidence, in Arabic and English.
  • «Make cards» from the ayah, the answer, a Name of Allah, a saying of the Salaf and the lessons, in three styles and three sizes.
  • A page for each of the Names of Allah, today’s cards, and the next occasion under the Hijri date.

The iPhone app’s update also brings what only an app can do: prayer alerts in the sound you choose, the reminders, the countdown on the Lock Screen, and the app icons.

What you find in it

  • Ask: in your language; the answer is a text from the Quran or the hadith, letter for letter, with its reference and a link to its page in the source. When no text matches your question it says so, and it never makes up a text of its own.
  • The Mushaf: with the tafsirs al-Muyassar and as-Sa’di, the meanings of the ayat in many languages from published translations, and recitations by human reciters.
  • Hadith: from the Encyclopedia of Translated Prophetic Hadiths, with its grade, explanation and lessons.
  • Your day: prayer times, the qibla and the Hijri calendar, computed on your device; with the adhkar, the tasbih, the Names of Allah and nearby mosques.

What we hold to

  • It does not give fatwas: fatwa questions and personal cases are referred to the competent authority.
  • It writes no religious text of its own: every ayah and hadith is copied from its source as it is, and the original Arabic always shows.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking: and your location stays on your device.
