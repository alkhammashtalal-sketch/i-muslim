What are the pillars of Islam?
Level A · Established Authentic hadith
«بُنِيَ الإِسْلاَمُ عَلَى خَمْسٍ: شَهَادَةِ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ، وَإِقَامِ الصَّلاَةِ، وَإِيتَاءِ الزَّكَاةِ، وَالْحَجِّ، وَصَوْمِ رَمَضَانَ»
Translation · reviewed

Islam is built upon five: the testimony that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, establishing the Salah, giving the Zakat, the Hajj, and fasting Ramadan.

Sahih al-Bukhari – Book of Faith – Hadith 8 Original source ↗
Plain explanation

The five pillars are the foundation of a Muslim's practice. Terms like Salah (prayer) and Zakat (obligatory charity) are kept in Arabic and explained rather than replaced.
